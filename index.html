About Accelerate-DAQ

Advancing Defense Research Through Convergence

Accelerate-DAQ strengthens North Carolina A&T State University's research capacity through an interdisciplinary framework connecting artificial intelligence, cyber defense, autonomous systems, and quantum technologies.

The project brings together faculty, postdoctoral scholars, graduate researchers, undergraduate researchers, research staff, facilities, and institutional resources to develop an integrated research ecosystem positioned for high-impact defense research.

Our Mission Build a sustainable university-wide research ecosystem that develops Research Champions, expands interdisciplinary research capacity, strengthens the STEM workforce, and advances technologies relevant to national defense.
