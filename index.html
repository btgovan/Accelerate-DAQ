About Accelerate-DAQ

Research Built for the Future.

Accelerate-DAQ is a university-wide research capacity-building initiative at North Carolina A&T State University focused on strengthening research excellence in artificial intelligence, cyber resilience, autonomous systems, and quantum technologies.

The initiative brings together faculty, postdoctoral scholars, graduate students, undergraduate researchers, research staff, centers, laboratories, and interdisciplinary expertise to create a coordinated ecosystem for discovery, training, prototype development, and workforce preparation.

Our Mission Build an integrated research ecosystem that advances transformational technologies, cultivates emerging Research Champions, strengthens interdisciplinary collaboration, and expands NC A&T's capacity for defense-relevant research.

Developing Research Leaders

The Research Champion Model.

Accelerate-DAQ develops research leadership at every stage of the academic research pipeline through mentoring, collaboration, technical development, and hands-on discovery.

RC-F

Faculty

Providing technical leadership, mentorship, proposal development, research integration, and interdisciplinary direction.

RC-P

Postdoctoral Scholars

Advancing cluster-based research, mentoring junior researchers, coordinating technical workflows, and accelerating dissemination.

RC-G

Graduate Researchers

Conducting dissertation-level research, publishing discoveries, developing proposals, and advancing mission-relevant innovation.

RC-U

Undergraduate Researchers

Gaining early hands-on experience in experimentation, computation, scientific communication, teamwork, and research discovery.
